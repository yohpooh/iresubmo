# Wassub!

## 📌 Overview
Wassub! is a modern subscription management application designed to help users track, organize, and manage all their recurring subscriptions in one convenient place. From entertainment platforms to productivity tools, Wassub! ensures you always know what you’re paying for, when payments are due, and how much you’re spending over time.

In today’s digital world, it’s easy to lose track of multiple subscriptions. Streaming services, cloud storage, AI tools, gaming memberships, and software plans can quickly pile up — leading to forgotten renewals, unexpected charges, and unnecessary expenses.

Wassub! solves this problem by giving users a centralized dashboard where they can:

- Monitor all active subscriptions
- Track billing cycles and renewal dates
- Receive timely reminders before payments are due
- Avoid unwanted auto-renewals
- Stay financially organized effortlessly

Whether you subscribe to platforms like Spotify, Netflix, YouTube Premium, Amazon Prime, Claude, ChatGPT, Disney+, or many others, Wassub! keeps everything streamlined and easy to manage.

## ✨ Features
### 📅 Subscription Tracking
Easily add and manage all your subscriptions in one place.
Track important details such as:

- Subscription name
- Monthly or yearly billing plan
- Cost and currency
- Renewal date
- Payment frequency
- Notes or custom details

##🔔 Smart Payment Reminders
Never get surprised by an automatic renewal again.
Wassub! sends timely reminders before upcoming billing dates so you can:

- Prepare your payments ahead of time
- Cancel subscriptions before renewal
- Avoid overdraft or unexpected charges
- Stay informed about upcoming expenses
